# 11. QR & Product UX Thinking

Rahul is interested in solving real-world UX problems rather than simply adding features.

One example is the problem of businesses needing separate QR codes for different outlets.

The desired experience is:

1. Customer scans one reusable QR.
2. The system determines the relevant outlet/context.
3. The customer should not be shown unrelated outlets.
4. The flow should require as little friction as possible.

This reflects a broader product philosophy:

> **Make the technology complex behind the scenes so the user experience stays simple.**
